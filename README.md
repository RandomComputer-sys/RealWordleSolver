This project is aimed to create a bot that solves the New York Times game, Wordle. 
At this moment in time i have only created the wordle game.

How To Use: run the Wordle_Visuals.ipynb file. You will see it give you the answer to the wordle.'
If you want it to not give you the answer edit out the print statement on line 11. When you press play 
you will be prompted to enter a 5 letter word at the top bar of the screen. You get 5 guesses to get the 
word right. Every guess will give you a zero, one, or two in each letter spot. A zero means the letter is not in the 
word, a 1 mean the letter is both in the word and in the right place, and a 2 means the letter is in the word but the 
wrong place.
before playing again, make sure the bar at the top of the screen is not asking for an input. If it is just click on 
the bar while the program is not running and press enter.
